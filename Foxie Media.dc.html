Questions, answered

What Foxie Media is, who runs it, and how to get in touch.

What is Foxie Media?

Foxie Media is a personal branding and brand management studio founded by Varsha Sangolli. It helps ambitious brands and founders build visibility, credibility and growth through strategy, storytelling and content.

Who founded Foxie Media?

Varsha Sangolli founded Foxie Media to be a creative partner for brands and founders that understands the internet, thinks strategically, cares about the idea, and can execute content properly.

What does Foxie Media do?

Foxie Media offers two core services: personal branding for founders and brand management for companies. Design, editing and AI creative are built into the work. Strategy comes first, then execution.

Is Foxie Media a social media marketing agency?

No. Foxie Media is not a traditional social media marketing agency. It is a strategy-first personal branding and brand management studio.

Who has Foxie Media worked with?

Selected work includes Toolandtea, Swishy AI, Mugdha Bhagchandka, LifeAt, Sindhu Biswal, Naiyya Saggi and Mihika. Results include Toolandtea growing from 0 to 10K Instagram followers in 30 days, Swishy AI from 0 to 15K in 30 days, and Mugdha Bhagchandka from 5K to 10K in 30 days.
